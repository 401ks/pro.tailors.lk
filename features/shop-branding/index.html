Shop Branding & Settings — a storefront your customers remember

Your logo, your colors, your receipts. Everything your customer sees carries your shop's name.

The problem today

Your workshop feels premium. Your receipts don't. Blank paper, no branding, no contact info — and no easy way to control what your customers see on tracking pages or your shop profile.

What it does

Upload your logo and cover once — they're auto-compressed to load fast. Every receipt, tracking page, and your public shop profile carry your name and identity. A preferences engine puts the details you tune most in one place: auto-save, public visibility, and notifications.

What's included

Auto-compressed media

Logo and cover photos compressed to under 100 KB.

Customizable receipts

Shop email, WhatsApp number, and a custom footer message.

Preferences engine

Auto-save, public visibility, and notification settings.

FAQ

Can I use my own logo on receipts and shop profile?

Yes. Upload your logo and cover photo, and they appear on receipts, tracking pages, and your public shop profile.

What if my logo photo is too large?

Uploads are auto-compressed to under 100 KB, so branding stays fast on any phone connection.

Can I customize what appears on receipts?

Yes. Set your shop email, WhatsApp number, and a custom footer message.

Can I control how public my shop is?

Yes. The preferences engine lets you toggle public visibility and other defaults.
