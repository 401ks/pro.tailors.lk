Financials & Invoicing — beautiful receipts, no loose ends

Branded, itemized PDF receipts in one click. Track advances and balances effortlessly.

The problem today

Who paid how much, and into which order? Advances are remembered, balances are forgotten, and hand-written receipts don't survive the rain or the year. Money leaks a little with every hole in the record.

What it does

Generate a branded, itemized PDF receipt in one click — your logo, your contact info, a custom footer. Log the advance when the order is placed. Subtotal, advance paid, and balance due are calculated for you, and every order carries a clear status: Unpaid, Partial Payment, or Fully Paid.

Unpaid / Partial Payment / Fully Paid on every order.

Who it's for

Any tailor or boutique that takes advances — which, in bespoke tailoring, is nearly everyone. Uniform makers invoicing schools, alterators who bill on pickup, and shops tired of chasing balances from memory all get the same clean record.

FAQ

How do advances and balances work?

Log the advance when the order is placed. Subtotal, advance paid, and balance due are auto-calculated in LKR.

Can I put my shop logo on receipts?

Yes. Receipts are branded with your logo, contact info, and a custom footer message.

What payment statuses exist?

Unpaid, Partial Payment, and Fully Paid — updated automatically as you log payments.

Is the receipt a PDF?

Yes. Every receipt is generated as a printable PDF in one click.
