Order Management & Production — from tape to track

A visual 6-step Kanban board that makes sure no order is left behind in the cutting room.

The problem today

Orders live in notebooks, on scraps of paper, and in your head. A suit gets cut, then sits forgotten for a week. A customer calls and no one can say where it is. Overdue orders cost you money — and eventually, customers.

What it does

Every order moves through six stages on a drag-and-drop board: Inbound → Cutting → Stitching → QC → Ready → Completed. Set a deadline and Tailors.lk Pro flags it the moment it's at risk. Everything about the order — customer, garments, quantities, notes — lives in one details hub.

What's included

Visual Kanban board

Drag-and-drop orders between production stages.

6-step workflow

Inbound → Cutting → Stitching → QC → Ready → Completed.

Smart deadlines

Automated overdue alerts before promises break.

Order details hub

Customer info, garments, quantities, and internal notes in one view.

How it works

01

Create the order

Attach a customer, pick the garments, set the deadline.

02

Drop it on the board

It lands in Inbound and flows forward as work happens.

03

Move as it progresses

Cutting → Stitching → QC. One tap per stage.

04

Get alerted

Deadlines approach, overdue orders get flagged automatically.

Who it's for

Ladies and gents tailors juggling many orders, boutiques handling production and alterations, uniform makers working to school deadlines, and any shop that has ever lost a garment in the back room. If you take more than a handful of orders a week, the board pays for itself in what you stop losing.

FAQ

What are the 6 production stages?

Inbound, Cutting, Stitching, QC, Ready, and Completed. Every order moves through the same visual board.

What happens when an order is overdue?

Smart deadlines trigger automated overdue alerts, so nothing sits forgotten in the cutting room.

Can I see everything about one order in one place?

Yes. The order details hub shows customer info, garments, quantities, and internal notes in a single view.

Is the Kanban board mobile-friendly?

Yes. Tailors.lk Pro is mobile-first, so the board works on the phone in your pocket, not just at a desk.
