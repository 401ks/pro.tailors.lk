Customer Tracking & Shop Profiles — “Is my suit ready?” ends today

Send a single link. Customers track their suit's progress and download their receipt — no app download required.

The problem today

Every order brings the same two interruptions: “Is my suit ready?” calls and “Can you send me the receipt?” requests. Cutters stop. You run to check. And every call eats minutes from your day.

What it does

Each order gets a private tracking link on its own page. Share it by SMS or WhatsApp and your customer — with no login and no app — can see exactly where their order is and download their branded receipt. Optionally, your shop also gets a public storefront profile with your logo and contact info.

What's included

Private tracking links

Share by SMS or WhatsApp. No customer login, no app download.

Public shop profiles

Toggleable storefront with branding, cover photo, and contact.

Live progress

Customers see which production stage their order is at.

Receipt download

Customers fetch their branded receipt from the same page.

FAQ

Do customers need an account to track an order?

No. Tracking links are private and work without any login or app download.

How do I send the tracking link?

Share it by SMS or WhatsApp directly from your order — one tap.

Are the tracking links secure?

Yes. Each link is private and unique to its order, so only the customer you share it with can view it.

Can customers download their receipt from the link?

Yes. The page shows the order progress and a one-tap branded receipt download.

What is a public shop profile?

An optional, toggleable storefront with your branding, cover photo, and contact info.
