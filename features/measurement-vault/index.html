Digital Measurement Vault — never measure the same customer twice

Every client's exact fit, stored on their profile with version history for their changing body.

The problem today

His fit is in a notebook. Her fit is on a loose sheet. When they come back a year later, the book is gone, the page is torn, or the handwriting has faded. So you measure again, guess, and hope.

What it does

The vault stores customer profiles — contact info and addresses — with measurement versioning built in. Save today's fit, and when it changes next year, save a new version. Garment-specific templates for shirts, suits, trousers, and traditional wear mean you record exactly the right fields every time.

What's included

Customer profiles

Contact info and addresses attached to every fit.

Measurement versioning

Multiple profiles per customer, with full history.

Garment templates

Shirts, suits, trousers, and traditional wear fields.

How it works

01

Add the customer

02

03

Save once, at the fitting. Done.

Their body changed — save the new fit with history intact.

Who it's for

Ladies and gents tailors with recurring customers, boutiques that fit bridal and formal wear, and any shop where a customer's name rings a bell but their measurements are a mystery. If customers come back even twice, the vault is worth it.

FAQ

What happens when a customer gains or loses weight?

Save a new version. The vault keeps every fit, so you can compare the latest against past ones.

How do garment-specific templates work?

Shirts, suits, trousers, and traditional wear each have their own field sets, so you record exactly what that garment needs.

Can one household share a profile?

Yes. Keep individual measurement profiles under each customer record, with history for every member.

Is the measurement data secure?

Yes. Measurements live inside private customer profiles, visible only to your shop.
