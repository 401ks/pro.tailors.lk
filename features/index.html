Everything a modern tailor shop runs on

Five product groups, built for the way Sri Lankan bespoke tailoring actually works. Every feature below is live, shipped, and free to use.

Order Lifecycle & Production

  • Visual Kanban board with drag-and-drop
  • 6-step workflow: Inbound → Cutting → Stitching → QC → Ready → Completed
  • Smart deadlines with automated overdue alerts
  • Order details hub — garments, quantities, notes
Explore Order Management →

Digital Measurement Vault

  • Customer profiles with contact info and addresses
  • Measurement versioning with history
  • Garment-specific templates: shirts, suits, trousers, traditional wear
  • Never measure the same customer twice
Explore the Vault →

Financials & Invoicing

  • Branded one-click PDF receipts with logo and footer
  • Advance Paid and Balance Due auto-calculated
  • Status badges: Unpaid / Partial Payment / Fully Paid
Explore Invoicing →

Customer Tracking & Shop Profiles

  • Private tracking links — share by SMS or WhatsApp
  • No customer login or app download needed
  • Public shop profiles with branding and contact
  • Customers see progress and download receipts
Explore Tracking →

Shop Branding & Settings

  • Auto-compressed logo & cover uploads (under 100 KB)
  • Customizable receipts: shop email, WhatsApp, footer
  • Preferences engine: auto-save, visibility, notifications
Explore Branding →
