Perfect Tailor Management System vs Tailors.lk Pro — Decoding the Price Tag

The Perfect Tailor Management System (PTMS) is a well-documented product with one unresolved case: its price. Three separate figures circulate in public sources. We treat this like an investigation — lay out the evidence, flag what is not in dispute, and open our own file completely.

Last verified: August 8, 2026 · Sources: public materials only, nothing estimated.

The Mystery

The Perfect Tailor Management System (PTMS) describes itself as a comprehensive solution that "manages customer measurement, orders, finances, and deliveries from one place." Its feature documentation is impressively granular — job management, purchase and inventory management, a billing system, an e-sketch book with album and styles management, and task management.

Then you try to find out what it costs, and the evidence gets strange.

Three Prices, One Product

Across public sources, three different monthly figures appear for PTMS:

  • $10/month — described alongside a "Free Version"
  • $25/month — cited in one comparison source
  • $99/month — cited in another comparison source

Reading the spread

A spread from $10 to $99 is not a rounding difference. The most charitable reading: PTMS runs multiple plans, tiers, or regional offers, and third-party comparison sites have each captured a different slice.

The practical consequence: you cannot budget for PTMS from public information alone. You must contact the vendor and ask. We state this not to attack PTMS but to arm you — whatever they quote, get it in writing, and ask what is included at each tier.
