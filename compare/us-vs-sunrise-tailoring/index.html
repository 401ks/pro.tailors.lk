Sunrise Tailoring vs Tailors.lk Pro — Eight Questions to Ask Before You Choose

Sunrise Tailoring markets itself as a cloud-based management and accounting system for tailoring shops, promising to "streamline your tailoring business" and manage "orders, inventory, and customer experience effortlessly." Its documented strengths lean toward the back office: customer management, measurements tracking, invoices, and payroll.

Before you choose it — or any tailoring software — put it through these eight questions. We will answer each one for Sunrise based on its public materials, and for ourselves too.

Last available: August 8, 2026 · Sources: public materials only, nothing estimated.

Question 1: What does it cost?

Sunrise Tailoring: The documented answer is "Price Available on Request." Multiple sources direct buyers to click through for a customized quote. There is no published figure.

Tailors.lk Pro: Free to start. No card, no quote request, no sales call. Published, on the pricing page, today.

Question 2: Can I try before I commit?

Sunrise Tailoring: No free tier is documented in public materials.

Tailors.lk Pro: The core product is free — not a trial. The order board, measurement vault, receipts, and tracking links carry no countdown.

Question 3: What currency will I pay in?

Sunrise Tailoring: The documented pricing signals are India-based (quote-driven, INR context).

Tailors.lk Pro: LKR native. Receipts, balances, and every figure your shop sees are built in rupees.

Question 4: Does it track my production floor?

Sunrise Tailoring: Its documented top features emphasize worker wage rates, work allocation, and process management — strong workforce and financial control.

Tailors.lk Pro: A visual Kanban board moves each order through Inbound, Cutting, Stitching, QC, Ready, and Completed, with automatic alerts when a deadline passes. Different lens, same goal: nothing gets lost.

Question 5: What happens with customer measurements?

Sunrise Tailoring: "Maintain Customer Profile" and "Customer Measurements" appear in its documented feature lists.

Tailors.lk Pro: A measurement vault with versioning — multiple profiles per customer with history — plus garment-specific templates for shirts, suits, trousers, and traditional wear.

Question 6: How do my customers find out their order is ready?

Sunrise Tailoring: Email/SMS capabilities are documented among its top features.

Tailors.lk Pro: Every order carries a private tracking link the customer opens in any browser — no account, no app. Automated WhatsApp updates are in development.

Question 7: Can my customers pay me through it?

Sunrise Tailoring: No local Sri Lankan payment gateway integration is documented.

Tailors.lk Pro: Payment tracking (advance, balance, status badges) is live now. Digital payment logging — Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, LankaQR — is in development, with PayHere-class gateway integration on the roadmap.

Question 8: Does it speak my language?

Sunrise Tailoring: No Sinhala or Tamil support is documented.

Tailors.lk Pro: Sinhala and Tamil are planned as core, not an afterthought. Join the waitlist to push them forward.

Give Credit Where Earned

Sunrise Tailoring's payroll and wage-rate depth is real. A workshop paying piece-rate tailors will find genuine value in "Worker Wise Wage Rate" and "Work Allocation and Collection." If workforce accounting is your primary headache and you are comfortable with quote-only pricing, it deserves a conversation.
