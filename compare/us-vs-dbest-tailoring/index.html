DBest Tailoring vs Tailors.lk Pro — A Comparison With the Lights On

Some comparisons are easy because everything is public. This one is not. So before any feature talk, we are turning the lights on: we will only compare what can be verified, and we will tell you exactly where the information runs out.

Our Promise on This Page

Some comparisons are easy because everything is public. This one is not — so before any feature talk, here is our commitment: we will only compare what can be verified, and we will tell you exactly where the information runs out. You deserve that from us, because it is the same standard we hold ourselves to on our own pricing page.

What Is Documented About DBest Tailoring

DBest Tailoring is described in public materials as "an all-in-one SaaS application for tailors and boutique owners to manage customer measurements, orders, and invoices." Its documented key features are:

  • Customer Management
  • Measurements Management
  • Order Management
  • Inventory Management
  • Financial Reporting

That is a credible, well-scoped feature list. It covers the same ground any serious tailoring system must.

What Is Not Documented

Now the honest gaps in the public record:

  • Pricing — not publicly disclosed. Public sources reference DBest in comparison tables without listing a figure. You would need to contact the vendor directly.
  • Currency and market — not clearly established in the materials we verified.
  • Free tier or trial — not documented.
  • Sinhala / Tamil support — not documented.
  • Local payment gateway integration (PayHere, Genie, Koko) — not documented.

None of this is a criticism. Plenty of solid products run quiet, sales-led pricing. But as a buyer, "not documented" means "you must ask," and asking costs you time and leverage.

What We Publish, So the Comparison Is Fair

To make this page useful rather than one-sided, here is everything about us, in the same format:

  • Pricing: published — free to start, on our pricing page
  • Free tier: the core product itself — 6-step order board with overdue alerts, versioned measurement vault with garment templates, branded LKR PDF receipts with advance/balance, private customer tracking links, public shop profile
  • Currency: LKR context
  • Trial: no countdown on core features
  • Sinhala / Tamil: planned — publicly listed on our roadmap
  • Local payments: tracking live now; digital payment logging (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, LankaQR) in development; PayHere-class gateway integration on the roadmap
  • Everything we have not built yet: labeled Coming Soon sitewide, each attached to a waitlist

Why This Matters

When a comparison page hides what it does not know, you make decisions on marketing. When it shows you the edges of the evidence, you make decisions on facts. If DBest's team gives you pricing and details we could not verify, weigh them — and weigh them against a product that publishes everything, including what it has not built yet.

Inventory management is the one documented DBest capability we do not match today. It is on our roadmap, and the waitlist moves it.
