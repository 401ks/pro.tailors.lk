FATbit Stitch vs Tailors.lk Pro — You May Be Comparing Two Different Tools

FATbit Stitch and Tailors.lk Pro mostly solve different jobs. One builds the online storefront where customers design custom garments. The other runs the physical shop that must actually produce them. Both are real products. Only one is built for your market.

Last verified: August 8, 2026 · Sources: public materials only, nothing estimated.

First, a quick detour

If you searched for a FATbit Stitch alternative, stop for one moment and check which problem you are actually solving — because FATbit Stitch and Tailors.lk Pro mostly solve different ones, and choosing between them starts with knowing the difference.

FATbit Stitch is a storefront builder for selling custom clothing online. Tailors.lk Pro is shop-management software for the physical tailor shop. Neither replaces the other out of the box.

What FATbit Stitch actually is

FATbit Stitch is documented as an "Online Custom Tailoring Platform" — a ready-to-use solution for businesses that want to sell custom clothing online. Its documented feature world revolves around the shopper's journey:

  • Fabric choice and style selection
  • Personalization
  • 3D visualization
  • Multiple payment methods
  • Inbuilt blog and advanced search

In short: it builds the storefront where a customer designs and orders a custom shirt or suit.

What It Is Not

FATbit Stitch is not documented as day-to-day shop operations software. Its own positioning does not attribute to it the measurement vault workflows, production stage tracking, or receipt/advance-balance management that a physical tailor shop runs on every day. (Some aggregator sites list generic tailoring features against it, but its own positioning is squarely the online custom-ordering experience.)

The pricing reality

Pricing is quote-only — multiple sources direct buyers to request a customized quote, with no public figure and no documented free trial. This is enterprise e-commerce territory.

The Two Jobs, Side by Side

One question up front decides almost everything: are you selling online, are you producing in a shop — or both?

The job Who does it
Let customers design & order custom garments onlineFATbit Stitch — that is its core
Track a garment from cutting to pickupTailors.lk Pro — 6-step board with overdue alerts
Store and version customer measurementsTailors.lk Pro — versioned vault, garment templates
Issue LKR receipts with advance/balanceTailors.lk Pro — branded one-click PDFs
Let customers check order status without callingTailors.lk Pro — private tracking links
Run an online customization storefront with 3D visualizationFATbit Stitch

So Which One Do You Need?

You probably need FATbit Stitch if

your business model is online custom clothing sales — customers configuring garments on a website, choosing fabrics, seeing 3D previews — and you have an enterprise budget with quote-based procurement.

You probably need Tailors.lk Pro if

you run a physical tailor shop or boutique where the daily reality is measurements, deadlines, advances, and customers asking when their order will be ready. That is the entire product: a free, mobile-first command center with a production board, a versioned measurement vault, branded LKR receipts, and self-service tracking links.

You might eventually want both

a storefront to take online custom orders, and an operations system to actually produce them. On our side, that future is visible — public shop profiles are live now, and a deeper storefront experience sits on the roadmap.

No Wrong Door

If FATbit Stitch is the tool you need, build with it — the online custom-tailoring market deserves good platforms. If the job is running the shop itself, we are ready today, free, and built for Sri Lanka.
