HandySeam vs Tailors.lk Pro — The Quick Answers

HandySeam is a real app with a free door and a ₦-priced Premium plan. Tailors.lk Pro is free for Sri Lankan tailor shops. Here is the quick, honest version.

Verified: August 8, 2026 · Sources: public materials only, nothing estimated.

The 30-second version

HandySeam is a real app for fashion designers and tailors, focused on customer management, measurement tracking, order history, and sales management. It has a free plan and a paid Premium plan priced at ₦3,500 per month — Nigerian Naira. Tailors.lk Pro is free, priced in LKR context, and built for Sri Lankan shops. Details below.

What is HandySeam?

An app that calls itself the tool to "simplify and supercharge" business operations for fashion designers and tailors. Documented features: customer information management, order history, invoicing, sales management, and a 360-degree design view.

Is HandySeam free?

Partially. Its documented free plan covers the essentials — customer management, order history, invoicing. The Premium plan is documented at ₦3,500/month. One third-party source cites $6/month instead; the figures differ, so check current pricing with HandySeam directly.

So what is the catch for Sri Lanka?

The currency. ₦ is Nigerian Naira. HandySeam's Play Store listing and pricing point at the Nigerian market. You would be paying for software priced and shaped for a different country's tailoring economy.

What does Tailors.lk Pro do that HandySeam does not document?

The live production board (Inbound → Cutting → Stitching → QC → Ready → Completed) with overdue alerts, branded receipts with advance/balance math, and customer tracking links. HandySeam's 360-degree design view is its own flourish — we do not document an equivalent, and we say so.

What does HandySeam have that you do not?

That 360-degree design view, for one — a feature aimed at the design side of fashion work. If visualizing designs is central to how you sell, weigh that honestly.

If I start free with you, when do I pay?

There is no billing today. When paid tiers arrive, they will be announced openly, and early users will be consulted and grandfathered. That commitment lives on our pricing page.

One line to decide?

HandySeam serves Nigeria. Tailors.lk Pro serves you. Both have free doors; only one is built around LKR, Sri Lankan customers, and the way your shop actually runs.
