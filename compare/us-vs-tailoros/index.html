TailorOS vs Tailors.lk Pro — Two Tools, Two Different Worlds

TailorOS is a premium operating system for high-end bespoke studios. Tailors.lk Pro is a free operating system for working Sri Lankan tailor shops. Both are real products. Only one is built for your market.

Last verified: August 8, 2026 · Sources: public materials only, nothing estimated.

What TailorOS actually is

TailorOS describes itself as a "business management application for tailoring studios" that "brings bookings, customer measurements, garment orders, fittings, payments, inventory, and client communication into one secure workspace." Its stated audience is explicit: bespoke ateliers, alteration chains, bridal studios, menswear houses, and made-to-measure brands.

Architecturally it is an offline-first system with Google Sign-In restricted to administrative authentication. It ships as six modules:

  • Studio Launch System — website, booking, enquiry funnel
  • Measurement CRM — vaults, garment history, repeat-order memory
  • Order Automation — job cards, stages, deposits, handoffs
  • Fitting Calendar — multi-location scheduling
  • Fabric Inventory — roll tracking, supplier records, low-stock
  • Revenue Analytics — turnaround, average order value, capacity

The pricing reality

TailorOS publishes no price. Its own terms state pricing is "shown in your order form, proposal, checkout flow, or subscription plan" — the purchase path runs through a demo call. For a tailor in Kurunegala, the first question any buyer asks ("what will this cost me?") has no public answer.

Where TailorOS genuinely excels

Credit where it is due. The module architecture is sophisticated. Offline-first design matters where connectivity is unreliable. The repeating-order memory and the fitting calendar's no-show reduction are mature features. For a bridal studio in Colombo's premium segment, this is a serious contender.

Where it misses the Sri Lankan majority

  • No published pricing — budget planning requires a sales conversation.
  • Premium positioning aimed at ateliers, not the average shop.
  • No documented Sinhala or Tamil support in public materials.
  • No documented PayHere, Genie, or Koko integration.

If multi-location booking is your single most urgent need, TailorOS may serve you better right now.
