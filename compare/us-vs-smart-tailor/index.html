Smart Tailor vs Tailors.lk Pro — Twelve Rounds

Smart Tailor tells tailors to "replace your paper notebooks and registers" — and we agree with that mission completely. It is a comprehensive, web-based solution for managing orders, measurements, workers, and billing, documented at $89/month for 5 users with no annual contracts.

Same mission. Different corners. Let us score it round by round.

Last verified: August 8, 2026 · Sources: public materials only, nothing estimated.

Round 1: The Entry Fee

Tailors.lk Pro

Smart Tailor: No annual contracts — monthly flexibility is real and fair.

Us: LKR 0. Not a discounted first month. Zero.

Round goes to: Tailors.lk Pro. $89/month buys a lot of thread. Free buys all of it.

Round 2: The Notebook Replacement

Draw

Smart Tailor: A Smart Measurement Log, webcam fabric capture, and a unified customer CRM. It swings hard here.

Us: A measurement vault protected with version history — every iteration of a customer's fit preserved — plus garment-specific templates for shirts, suits, trousers, and traditional wear.

Round goes to: Draw on ambition. Both throw real punches at the paper notebook. Wear both and keep what feels right.

Round 3: Production Visibility

Tailors.lk Pro

Smart Tailor: Order management is documented; stage-by-stage production detail is not specified in public materials.

Us: Six explicit stages — Inbound, Cutting, Stitching, QC, Ready, Completed — on a drag-and-drop board, with automatic overdue alerts when a deadline slips.

Round goes to: Tailors.lk Pro, on documented detail.

Round 4: The Money Round

Tailors.lk Pro

Smart Tailor: Billing is documented among its core features.

Us: Branded one-click PDF receipts with advance paid, balance due, auto-calculated subtotals, and status badges — Unpaid, Partial, Fully Paid. Digital payment logging (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, LankaQR) is in development.

Round goes to: Tailors.lk Pro for today's depth; the fight continues when payment logging lands.

Round 5: The Customer's Corner

Tailors.lk Pro

Smart Tailor: Customer management documented; customer self-service not specified.

Us: Every order gets a private tracking link. Customers check status and download receipts without accounts, logins, or phone calls.

Round goes to: Tailors.lk Pro. The customer experience is a documented weapon.

Round 6: Language

Smart Tailor — today

Smart Tailor: Multi-language support is documented — a genuine strength.

Us: Sinhala and Tamil are planned as core. Planned is not shipped, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Round goes to: Smart Tailor, today. Join our waitlist and help flip this round.

Round 7: Barcode

Smart Tailor — today

Smart Tailor: Barcode stock tracking is documented.

Us: On the roadmap, not live.

Round goes to: Smart Tailor, today.

Round 8: Home Turf

Tailors.lk Pro

Smart Tailor: Priced in USD, shaped for a global audience.

Us: Built in Sri Lanka, for LKR, around PayHere/Genie-class rails (on the roadmap) and WhatsApp habits your customers already have.

Round goes to: Tailors.lk Pro. Home advantage is real.

The Judges' Decision

4–2–1, Home Team

Smart Tailor is a legitimate, feature-rich contender with monthly flexibility and documented extras like webcam fabric capture. At $89/month for five users, it is priced for shops with steady revenue and international billing comfort.

Tailors.lk Pro wins on the two rounds that decide most Sri Lankan shops: the entry fee and the home turf. Everything core — the board, the vault, the receipts, the tracking links, the shop profile — is live and free. The rest of the card is visible on the roadmap, and the waitlist decides what steps into the ring next.
