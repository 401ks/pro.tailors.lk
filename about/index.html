Our Story

Built for the tailors of Sri Lanka

Tailors.lk Pro exists because the tools a modern tailor shop actually needs — orders, measurements, receipts, customer updates — were never built in one place, in LKR, with WhatsApp at the center, and priced for a small shop.

Why paper notebooks fail

01

Everything lives in one notebook

A single register holds every order from every customer — shirts, trousers, sarongs, prom dresses. One lost page is lost receipts, lost advance balances, and a customer asking "where is my kurta?" with no answer.

02

WhatsApp is the memory

Order numbers, waist sizes, "please hurry" — all mixed in with family photos and jokes. The measurement that lives only in a chat thread dies when the phone is dropped, backed up, or just impossible to find three months later.

03

Sizes change; records don't

A customer's fit changes across shops and seasons. Renovating the last pair of trousers means re-measuring from scratch every single time — and praying the tailor remembers the seams.

Generic CRMs fail tailors

A CRM built for a consulting firm assumes deals, pipelines, and quarterly revenue. A tailoring shop has fitting charts, cutters, and a deadline.

Tailors.lk Pro is the opposite: a free, mobile-first system designed around how a tailor shop actually runs — measurement first, order second, and the customer kept in the loop without a single phone call.

No sales pipeline. No generic "accounts." No enterprise onboarding. Just the board, the vault, the receipt, and the tracking link.

What we believe

Professional tools, small-shop prices

Enterprise software is priced for corporates. The best tools for tailor shops have been priced like a second mortgage — until now. Free to start, always.

Built for Sri Lanka, not adapted

LKR, Sinhala and Tamil support, PayHere and Genie from day one, WhatsApp-native flows. Local tailoring is not a checkbox in a global product.

Honest about the roadmap

We publish what is live and what is next on a public roadmap. If we're honest about what exists, we stay honest about what doesn't — no fake availability.

In one word: built here

Every screen is designed around a Sri Lankan shop — the 10.00 am walk-in, the "sizes needed tomorrow" pressure, and the customer who doesn't want to learn a new app just to check a status.

Start free, stay free

The free core replaces the notebook — no card, no trial countdown. Join the early-access list and be first when it opens.
