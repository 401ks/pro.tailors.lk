Set up in under 10 minutes

From tape measure to tracking link in four steps

Your shop, your phone, your routine — just upgraded. Here's exactly how running an order works.

01

Create your shop & add a customer

Sign in free, name your shop, and add your first customer — name, phone, and address.

What you see

A simple customer profile with their details saved.

What your customer sees

Nothing yet — they're just in your book.

02

Save measurements with garment templates

Pick the garment — shirt, suit, trouser, traditional wear — and the right fields appear. Save the fit once, version it as their body changes.

What you see

Their measurements in the vault, ready to reuse.

What your customer sees

The confidence that you remember them.

03

Create the order & move it through the 6-step board

Inbound → Cutting → Stitching → QC → Ready → Completed. Drag it forward as work happens, and set the deadline so it never sits forgotten.

What you see

A live board of everything in production.

What your customer sees

Their order progressing toward "Ready."

04

Send the tracking link & branded receipt

One tap. Your customer gets a private link to watch the order and download their receipt — no login, no app.

What you see

A clean, branded order closed out.

What your customer sees

A premium tracking page with their receipt.
