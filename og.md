## STYLEGUIDE

### Brand tone / personality

The site uses a **Neo-Brutalist** aesthetic — "Sharp Local Business Partner." Unapologetic honesty, raw structural elements, high-impact visual hierarchy. No soft corporate gradients; instead high-contrast intersections, heavy strokes, and a "paper-to-digital" utilitarianism. The interface communicates speed and structural integrity, as tactile and dependable as a tailor's measuring tape.

**Key stylistic pillars:**
- **Honest structure** — visible 3px borders and grid lines are the primary decoration.
- **Aggressive contrast** — deep black (`#1A1A1A`) against warm cream (`#FFFDF7`) for maximum legibility and brand recall.
- **Physicality** — hard, non-blurred offset shadows; buttons "press down" on interaction.
- **Local utility** — sized for clarity on mid-range mobile devices; functional density over decorative whitespace.

### Typography

- **Headlines & labels:** **Space Grotesk**, Bold / **700**, set **UPPERCASE** (brutalist "poster" aesthetic).
- **Body text:** **Inter**, weights 400–600, for legibility on mobile.
- **Trust lines** ("Free to start"): `label-bold` — Space Grotesk 700, 14px, letter-spacing 0.05em.
- **Numbers / currency (LKR):** high-weight typography so financial data scans instantly.
- Display scale: ~64px desktop (clamp down to ~40px mobile), line-height 1.1, letter-spacing −0.02em.

### Visual motifs

- **Hard corners:** 0px border radius on all containers, buttons, cards, inputs. Circles reserved for human/avatar/WhatsApp iconography only.
- **Stroke uniformity:** 3px black border on all primary UI components.
- **Hard shadow:** `3px 3px 0px #1A1A1A` (solid, non-blurred) on all elevated elements.
- **Dot-grid background:** 24px spacing, `#E5E5E5` dots on cream.
- **8px spacing base;** generous component padding (24–32px).
- **Icons:** inline stroke SVG, 24×24 viewBox, `stroke-width="1.8"`, `stroke-linecap/linejoin=round`.
- **Imagery style:** no photography on the site — flat vector composition, geometric shapes, poster-like typographic layouts.
- **Active states:** press-down — `translate(2px, 2px)` with shadow reduced to `1px 1px 0px`.

### Do / Don't

- **Do:** use bright orange for the single primary action; use cream as the dominant field; use black 3px borders and hard shadows; set headlines uppercase in Space Grotesk 700; keep shapes geometrically sharp.
- **Do:** treat green as exclusive to WhatsApp / "paid" states and red exclusively for overdue/errors — never as decorative colors.
- **Don't:** use rounded corners, soft/blurred shadows, gradients, glassmorphism, or photos of stock models.
- **Don't:** overcrowd — brutalist composition wants generous white space around heavy strokes.

### OG Image Art Direction

OG images should read as **flat, poster-like typographic cards in the same Neo-Brutalist language as the site**: a warm cream (`#FFFDF7`) 1200×630 canvas with a faint 24px dot-grid, a large white card containing the headline, framed by a thick `#1A1A1A` border and the signature hard shadow. The brand strip should be a solid bright-orange (`#FF6B35`) top band with `TAILORS.LK PRO` in Space Grotesk Bold uppercase white letter-spacing; the main headline in near-black Space Grotesk Bold uppercase; supporting line(s) in `#4A4A4A` Inter; and a single CTA pill (orange or WhatsApp-green) with black border and hard shadow in the bottom-right. A simple geometric vector motif relevant to the page (kanban column, measuring tape, receipt, chat bubble) may sit left of the headline, always stroke-style with `#1A1A1A` lines — never photographic, never rounded. Every image must remain legible at 1200×630 and down to small link-preview sizes.
