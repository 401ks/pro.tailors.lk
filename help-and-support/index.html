Frequently asked

Is Tailors.lk Pro really free?+

Yes. The core — the measurement vault, order boards, receipts and a tracking link — is free to start, with no card. You choose to pay only if you ever want an advanced tier, and the roadmap is public about what that looks like.

What languages does Tailors.lk Pro support?+

The public roadmap lists Sinhala and Tamil as day-one-supported for the platform, alongside English. We're building toward that deliberately — a tool that ignores Sri Lanka's languages isn't built for Sri Lanka.

Does it work on my phone?+

Yes. Tailors.lk Pro is mobile-first — you run your whole shop from a phone in your pocket. And your customers never need an app: the tracking link opens in any browser.

Are receipts in Sri Lankan Rupees?+

Yes. Receipts are LKR documents with advances and balances shown clearly, ready for your shop's branding. Try the live invoice generator now for a feel of it.

How does my customer check order status?+

Each order has a private tracking link. When a step is updated on your board the customer can open that link to see the move — no login, no new app to learn.
