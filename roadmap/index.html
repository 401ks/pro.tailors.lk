The vision

Where this is heading — planned, no dates, and no hype. If any of it matters to your shop, tell us.

Agentic AI & Automation

  • AI WhatsApp concierge — answers "Where is my suit?" and books fittings.
  • Photo-to-measurement AI — front + side photos → base measurements.
  • Auto-scheduling agent — assigns orders by workload, skill, and deadline.
  • Smart fabric recommender — AI suggestions from history and events.
  • Context-aware AI workspaces — AI drafts invoices and design notes from shop data.

MCP — "Talk to Your Shop"

  • MCP integration — analytics via Claude/ChatGPT or local LLMs.

PaaS & Ecosystem

  • Headless API — REST/GraphQL for ERP, POS, QuickBooks, Xero.
  • White-label franchise tier — run your own brand on the platform.
  • Multi-branch management — transfer orders between branches.
  • Fabric & inventory supply chain — barcode rolls, suppliers, auto-reorder.

Team & HR

  • Piece-rate payroll — auto-calculated tailor commissions.
  • Role-based access control — Front Desk, Cutters, Master Tailors.
