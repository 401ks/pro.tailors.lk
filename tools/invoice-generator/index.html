How to write a tailor shop invoice in Sri Lanka

A clear invoice protects both you and your customer. In Sri Lankan tailoring, most shops work on advances: a customer pays part of the price up front, the balance is settled at delivery. This generator calculates subtotals, shows the advance paid, and tells you the balance due — in LKR, automatically.

What every tailor receipt should show

  • Your shop name and an invoice number, so both sides can refer to the job later.
  • A line for each garment or service — e.g. "Trouser stitching", "Sari jacket", "School uniform alteration" — with quantity and price.
  • The date, because disputes about "when was this booked" usually start with a missing date.
  • The advance paid and the balance due. Writing both down is what turns an argument into a record.
